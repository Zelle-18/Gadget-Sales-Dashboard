# Gadget-Sales-Dashboard
Gadget Sales Dashboard is an interactive dashboard that provides a summary of the sales and transactions of a gadget store in 2026. It helps the business identify which products, brands, and categories, region and customer drive the most sales. The dashboard aims to support business strategy and data-driven decision-making.

**BUSINESS OBJECTIVES**
- Determine the overall revenue, total units sold, average order value, and total completed orders.
- Identify the sources of revenue.
- Identify the top-performing categories, brands, and products.
- Identify which shipping method is used for high-value transactions.
- Identify the most frequently used payment method.
- Determine which region has the largest customer count.
- Determine whether sales are growing month over month.
- Identify which products are frequently purchased together by customers.

**TOOLS AND TECHNOLOGIES**
Power BI – For data visualization, dashboard development, and DAX measures for calculating metrics.
PostgreSQL – For data cleaning, data extraction, and SQL-based data analysis.

**KEY KPIs**
Total Revenue
Total Units Sold
Average Order Value
Completed Orders
Category Revenue Percentage
Brand Revenue Percentage
Product Revenue Percentage

**DASHBOARD PAGES**
OVERVIEW – The Overview dashboard answers the question, “Where are the sales coming from?” It provides a general view of transactions, including monthly transactions, top-performing brands, customer segment revenue, revenue over time, and revenue distribution by shipping and payment methods.

CATEGORY – The Category dashboard provides an overview of transactions by category, including revenue percentage, total units sold, average order value, monthly revenue and units sold, category revenue contribution, and regional revenue.

BRAND – The Brand dashboard provides an overview of transactions by brand, including the top product per brand, brand revenue ranking, brand revenue by region, monthly sales, and purchase frequency.

PRODUCT – The Product dashboard answers the question, “Which products generate the most sales?” It shows the top products by units sold and total revenue, revenue by region, monthly revenue versus units sold, and product revenue versus units sold.

REGION – The Region dashboard provides stakeholders with an overview of regional transactions, including monthly customer count, regional revenue contribution, top-performing regions, top products by region, and monthly transactions.

CUSTOMER – The Customer dashboard provides an overview of customer segment transactions, including the top products, brands, and categories by units sold, customer segment revenue by brand, revenue contribution, monthly transactions, units sold, completed orders, and other customer-related metrics.


**INSIGHTS**
1. The Laptop category generated the highest overall revenue, contributing 33.91% of total revenue, despite not having the highest number of completed orders. This indicates that higher-priced product categories can generate more revenue even when they have fewer orders than lower-priced categories.

2. Accessories had the highest total unit sold during the period, indicating strong customer demand for lower-priced products. The category accounted for 55.17% of total units sold, showing that customers frequently purchase Accessories even though their average selling price is considerably lower than other categories.

3.Laptop products generated the highest average order value across the VIP, Premium, and Regular customer segments. This indicates that customers from all three segments tend to generate higher-value orders when purchasing Laptops.

4.The MacBook Air M5 13 + Sony WH-1000XM7 combination was the most frequently purchased product combination during the period, appearing together in 16 completed orders. At the category level, Laptop + Accessories was also the most frequent combination, while Tablet + Accessories and Accessories + Accessories had the fewest completed orders among the analyzed combinations.

5. VIP, Premium, and Regular customers all showed strong purchasing activity in the Accessories category. The analysis also identified strong demand for Logitech and Anker products, particularly among Premium and VIP customers.

6.The business has a high concentration of revenue across a relatively small number of products, brands, and customers. The top 5 products contribute 51.98% of overall revenue, the top 5 brands contribute 70.24%, and the top 10 customers contribute 55.13%. This indicates that the business relies heavily on a limited number of revenue sources, creating potential concentration risk if demand for major products, brands, or customers declines.


**RECOMMENDATION**
1. Continue prioritizing the Laptop category in marketing and promotional campaigns because it generates the highest revenue contribution. The business could create product-focused advertisements, short promotional videos, and feature comparisons highlighting the key benefits of Laptop products to attract more customers and potentially increase sales.

2.Since Accessories have the highest total unit sales, the business could explore bundling Accessories with Tablet and Smartwatch products, particularly because Tablet + Accessories and Smartwatch + Accessories have lower cross-selling activity than Laptop + Accessories and Phone + Accessories. Targeted bundles could encourage customers purchasing Tablets and Smartwatches to add complementary Accessories, potentially increasing revenue and average order value.

3. Create a loyalty program for customers who purchase higher-value products, allowing them to earn points that can be redeemed for discounts or rewards on future purchases. This could encourage repeat purchases and strengthen customer retention, particularly among Premium and VIP customers.

4. Use high-performing Laptop products as an opportunity to promote lower-selling Accessories. For example, the business could recommend lower-selling Accessories alongside popular Laptop products during checkout or through product bundles. This could increase exposure for slower-moving Accessories while maintaining the strong Laptop + Accessories cross-selling pattern.

5. Create exclusive bundles or targeted product recommendations for Premium and VIP customers based on their existing purchasing behavior. For VIP customers, promote the iPhone 16 Pro 256GB + Logitech MX Master 4 combination, while Premium customers can be targeted with the MacBook Air M5 13 + Sony WH-1000XM7 combination. These bundles are based on combinations already purchased together by each segment and could encourage additional purchases and increase future order value.

6. Collaborate with the Marketing and Sales teams to promote products and brands with lower orders and unit sales. These products could be targeted toward regions with larger customer bases, such as CALABARZON and NCR, through targeted promotions, product recommendations, or special offers. The goal is to increase awareness, orders, and unit sales of lower-performing products while reducing the business's dependence on a small number of products and brands.
